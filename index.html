    Help

    Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD steer. Down = straight. Space / Up = jump, and flip while airborne. F toggles fast mode. P pauses. R restarts.

    Mouse: the skier heads towards the pointer. Point above the skier to stop.

    Touch: hold a finger on the slope to steer towards it, or use the buttons.

    Tricks: hold left / right while airborne to spin (on touch, hold ◀ ▶ or a finger beside the skier). Land facing downhill: 360 = 100, 720 = 250, 1080 = 500. A flip is 50, and spin + flip in one jump scores ×1.5. Land crooked or mid-flip and you bail.

    Big ramps send you higher than small ones. Moguls bump you. You can clear small things while airborne, but not big trees or lift poles.

    Score = metres + trick points. Make the top ten and you get to enter your initials.

    Races: Multiplayer → create a race and share the link. Each time the leader passes 1000 m the yeti eats whoever is last; most points when eaten wins.
